02

Co-founder · RegTech

BIRDBench

A workbench of interoperable tools, extendable by AI coding assistants

BIRD Software Solutions provides BIRDBench, a tools workbench that brings together open and commercial interoperable extensions to the FreeBIRD application.

Because the FreeBIRD application is built on the ubiquitous, AI-friendly Django framework, developing your own custom extensions can be accelerated with AI coding assistants.

You can develop a tool that solves just the precise problem you want to solve and add it to the workbench, where it interoperates with other tools solving orthogonal problems.

01

Maintainer · GovTech

Swiss Law Collection

Swiss federal and cantonal law as a queryable dataset

A dashboard over Swiss federal and cantonal legislation, plus a static read-only JSON, CSV and SDMX API served straight from GitHub Pages. Legal domains are harmonised across the Confederation and all 26 cantons, so federal and cantonal acts can be compared on the same axis.

Statute published as data rather than as PDFs means downstream systems can be tested against it, and Atom and RSS feeds make a change to the law something software can subscribe to.

Coverage34,443 acts — Confederation and 26 cantons
Articles824,663 indexed
FormatsJSON · CSV · SDMX
SourcesFedlex (federal) · LexFind (cantonal)
VerificationPublished reconciliation against BADAC and chstat
Pipelinegithub.com/benjamin-arfa/swiss-law
Open dataSDMXJSON APIPython

03

Contributor · Open Legal Lab 2026

Law as Code

Swiss law turned into rules a computer can execute

Built at Open Legal Lab 2026, the Swiss legal-tech hackathon in Magglingen. Articles of Swiss tax and social-insurance law are pulled from Fedlex and turned into executable rules, so a question about contributions or entitlement can be answered by running the law itself instead of re-implementing it by hand in every administrative system.
