01 — The practice

Engineering that survives the audit

Arfa Digital Consulting turns data problems in regulated settings into working systems. It combines software development, data analysis and strategic consulting, tuned for Swiss and European rules.

Behind it is Benjamin Arfa: nine years across regulatory technology, healthcare and Life Sciences manufacturing, including metadata modelling for the ECB and the Swiss FSO. Today he is Technical Manager at HL7 Switzerland and co-founder of BIRDBench Ltd.

03

Contributor · RegTech

Eclipse FreeBIRD Tools

Tooling to democratise BIRD as an open standard

An open-source toolkit for the Banks' Integrated Reporting Dictionary, the standard that defines the rules and templates European banks use to report to supervisors. It manages dictionaries and mappings, and validates and transforms BIRD models.
