Technical Manager at HL7 Switzerland (FHIR-CH), driving healthcare data standardisation in Switzerland. HL7 sets the global standards for interoperability of health information technology; hl7.ch is the Swiss affiliate, and fhir.ch is where the Swiss FHIR implementation guides are published.
