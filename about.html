The founder

Benjamin Arfa

Data and software engineer in Basel. Nine years across healthcare, regulatory technology and enterprise governance — founder of Arfa Digital Consulting, co-founder of BIRDBench Ltd.

01 — Background

Professional background

I specialise in data integration, governance and lifecycle management across regulatory technology and healthcare. Over nine years I have built deep expertise in implementing data strategies and technical solutions that hold up under scrutiny.

In data strategy and architecture I focus on comprehensive lifecycle management through effective data modelling and technical implementation — designing robust, business-aligned systems while building data-driven organisational capability.

My technical work spans requirements engineering, bridging business and technical teams, and software engineering for scalable data applications. As Technical Manager at HL7 Switzerland (FHIR-CH) I help drive healthcare data standardisation. As co-founder of BIRDBench Ltd I bring innovation into RegTech, and as an Eclipse contributor I build regulatory tooling in the open.
